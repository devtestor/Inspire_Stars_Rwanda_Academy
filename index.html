About

A professional academy built for young athletes

Inspire Stars Academy is a youth sports development academy based in Kigali, Rwanda. We identify, develop, and support young athletes through professional coaching, structured competition, and a focus on education and personal growth.

We work alongside schools, families, communities, and international partners to give every athlete a clear pathway — from their first session to opportunities at the highest level, in Rwanda and abroad.

  • Talent identification
  • Professional coaching
  • International exposure
  • Education & life skills

Our Purpose

What we stand for

A clear set of principles guides how we coach, compete, and develop young people.

Mission

Developing talent

To develop young athletes through professional, age-appropriate coaching that builds technical skill, physical literacy, and a foundation for long-term success in sport and in life.

Vision

Building character

To be the leading youth sports academy in Rwanda — recognised for the quality of our coaching, the character of our athletes, and the opportunities we create.

Values

Creating opportunities

Discipline, respect, teamwork, and integrity. We hold our athletes and ourselves to high standards, and we work to open real doors — from local competition to the international stage.

Health & Education

The whole athlete

Sport is our foundation, not the whole picture. We promote healthy lifestyles, education, and the life skills that support every young person well beyond their playing years.

International Pathways

International exposure

International experience is a structured part of our program. Our athletes have already trained, competed, and learned abroad — including trips to England, Germany, and France — with more to come as we open doors to world-class environments.

Flagship Program

Manchester City study tour

In partnership with Trans World Educational Experiences, a group of our athletes aged 14–17 travelled to Manchester for a study tour at Manchester City's stadium and training facilities. They visited the Etihad Campus, learned from professional coaches, and observed elite teams at work.

Manchester, United Kingdom  ·  Ages 14–17  ·  Manchester City FC  ·  Trans World Educational Experiences

Our pathway approach: Local foundations → National development → International exposure → Professional aspirations

How the pathway works

1

Foundation

Athletes begin with structured, age-appropriate coaching in their chosen discipline at our Kigali base.

2

Development

Alongside training, athletes build discipline, leadership, and the habits that support high performance.

3

Competition

We connect athletes with competitive opportunities in Rwanda to build experience and a record of performance.

4

Exposure

Selected athletes access international study tours and partner programs abroad, experiencing the standards of the game at the highest level.

Our Team

Coaches & leadership

Experienced coaches committed to the development of every athlete.

Nzeye Rodrigue
Founder & CEO

Nzeye Rodrigue is the founder and CEO of Inspire Stars Academy, which he built to give young Rwandan athletes access to professional coaching and a clear pathway to develop. He leads the coaching team and the academy's international partnerships and travel programs.

Rutagengwa Joseph
Sports Director

Rutagengwa Joseph rose through our program from player to Sports Director, recognised for his leadership and dedication to young athletes. A FERWAFA-licensed coach, he has been selected for an advanced coaching course with Atlético Madrid.
